The idea behind Narrow Gate
The pursuit of beauty is a way of living in the world.

This journal brings together the work, interests, conversations, and collected curiosities of Vince Graham: places and ideas that invite us to question convention, learn from the past, and imagine more beautiful possibilities.

From neighborhood design to questions of law, culture, place, and the good life, Narrow Gate is a living record of things that matter.
